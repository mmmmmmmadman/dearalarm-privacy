Dear Alarm Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026

Overview

Dear Alarm lets you record a short voice message and send it to someone you have paired with. The message plays on their device as an alarm at the time you set. The app has no account system of its own, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

iCloud

Dear Alarm stores and transfers your data through Apple iCloud (CloudKit) using the iCloud account already signed in on your device. Voice messages and alarm times are written to a private iCloud database that you own and shared only with the people you pair with. MADZINE operates no server of its own and cannot read your private messages.

Voice Messages

A voice message is at most 30 seconds long. When the recipient chooses not to play it, or after it has been played, it is deleted. There is no way to recover a deleted message. Unpairing deletes the other person's messages from your device and removes their access to yours.

Microphone

Microphone access is used only while you hold the record button. Nothing is recorded in the background.

Display Name

The name you enter is stored on your device and shared only with the people you pair with. Your email address and Apple ID are never shown to other users.

Public Board

The Board is optional. Requests you post, voice replies you send and ratings you give are stored in a public iCloud database and are visible to other users of the app. You are identified there only by an anonymous iCloud record identifier, not by name or email. You can block any user, and you can report content to MADZINE for review.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple In-App Purchase. Dear Alarm receives only the purchase status from Apple and no payment details.

Children

Dear Alarm is intended for users aged 13 and over and does not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Deleting Your Data

Unpairing removes shared data from both devices. Deleting the app and its iCloud data from iCloud settings removes everything else. You can also email the address below to request removal of Board content.

Third Parties

Dear Alarm shares no data with third parties other than Apple, whose iCloud and In-App Purchase services it relies on.
